# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NotDefteri to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
do not follow this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly break it.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/olcayalkan). All reports are handled confidentially.
